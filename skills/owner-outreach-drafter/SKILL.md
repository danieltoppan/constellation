# Owner Outreach Drafter

Writes first-touch outreach to the owner or founder of a software company on behalf of a long-term, permanent-hold acquirer. Input is a thesis from `vms-acquisition-thesis` (or equivalent notes with sources). Output is drafts for the user to edit and send personally.

## Inputs

- The thesis or notes (required), including at least one sourced trigger
- Recipient name and title (required; if unknown, stop and ask, do not guess)
- Sender name and firm. If not given, use placeholders [Your name] and [Firm]
- Channel preference, if any. Default: email + call opener + follow-up note

## Rules

1. One real reason to write now, taken from a sourced trigger in the thesis. If there is no trigger, use a sourced fact about the business (years in the niche, product focus) and say in the notes that the reason is weak.
2. Only state facts that are in the thesis with a source. Never imply knowledge you do not have (revenue, plans, health, family).
3. No offer, no price, no "exit", "valuation", "deal", "synergies" or "partnership opportunity" in a first touch. The ask is a conversation.
4. Tone: brief, warm, plain, respectful of their time. Write the way a thoughtful person writes to a busy founder.
5. Email body 70 to 120 words. Subject line under 7 words, specific to them, no clickbait.
6. Banned openers and filler: "I hope this finds you well", "I came across your company", "quick question", "just following up", "circle back", "touch base", excessive compliments.
7. Do not mention a person by first name unless the thesis confirms their name and role.
8. No em dashes. Use commas, colons or parentheses.
9. Never send. Present drafts only. Remind the user to check consent rules where relevant (CASL in Canada, CAN-SPAM in the US) and their employer's outreach policy.

## Output

For each company:

**Email**
- Subject
- Body (greeting, who you are and how your firm owns companies in one line, the specific reason, the low-pressure ask, sign-off)

**Call opener** (2 to 3 sentences the user can say in under 15 seconds, ending with "Do you have two minutes, or is there a better time?")

**Three first-call questions** (ownership today; their 5 to 10 year view; what would matter most in a partner)

**Follow-up note** (day 10, 2 to 3 sentences, adds one new piece of value or context, not "bumping this")

**Cadence**

| When | Touch |
| --- | --- |
| Day 0 | Email (or printed letter for long-tenured founders) |
| Day 3 | Call |
| Day 10 | Follow-up note |
| Quarterly | A note tied to a new, real trigger |

**Verify before sending** (bullets: every name, title, date and fact used, each with its source link from the thesis)

## Final check

- Every fact traces to the thesis sources
- Word counts within limits
- No banned phrases, no em dashes
- The reason to write is specific to this owner, not reusable for any company
